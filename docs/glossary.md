# Glossary

Short definitions for email and newsletter terms used in this site.

> The longer historical glossary lives at [Improvements → Glossary](/docs/improvements/glossary) and is kept intact.

## ESP (Email Service Provider)

A platform used to send marketing or transactional email.

## Design system (email)

Shared React/HTML building blocks (typography, tables, layout) used to assemble templates consistently.

## Markdown-to-email

Pipeline that turns markdown (and front matter) into HTML email bodies.

## Deliverability

Whether messages reach the inbox versus spam/promotions; involves authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), reputation, and engagement.

## Transactional email

Automated messages triggered by user actions (receipts, resets), distinct from marketing newsletters.
